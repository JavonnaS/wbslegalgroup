We are committed to delivering exceptional legal services with integrity, empathy, and excellence. Our holistic approach ensures that we address not only your legal concerns, but the bigger picture— empowering you to move forward with confidence.

Meet the Attorneys

Javonna Smith, Esq.

Originally from St. Louis, Missouri, Javonna Smith brings a thoughtful, client-centered approach to every matter she handles.

She earned her Juris Doctorate from Golden Gate University School of Law, where she worked with and represented low-wage workers in employment disputes, strengthening her foundation in advocacy and practical legal strategy.

After law school, she began her legal career as a public defender handling felony cases in Fulton County. She has also practiced family law and judgment enforcement, giving her broad litigation and client counseling experience.

Shaugnassy Williams-Bryant, Esq.

From Jacksonville, Florida, Shaugnassy Williams-Bryant is known for strategic advocacy and compassionate client service.

She received her Juris Doctorate from John Marshall University. During law school, she gained meaningful experience in civil rights matters, shaping her commitment to service-driven legal representation.

After law school, she gained experience in criminal law, family law, and HOA enforcement. In her spare time, she has provided pro bono support to families with wills and trusts—experience that reflects the firm’s commitment to offering these important services.
