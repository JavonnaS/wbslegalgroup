Practice Areas

WBS Legal Group provides strategic legal representation to individuals and families in matters involving personal injury, estate planning, and traffic violations. Our attorneys combine advocacy, experience, and practical solutions to help clients protect their rights, plan for the future, and navigate legal challenges with confidence.

⚖

Personal Injury

An unexpected injury can disrupt your life, leaving you with medical expenses, lost wages, and uncertainty about what comes next. WBS Legal Group advocates for individuals who have suffered injuries due to the negligence of others, helping them protect their rights and pursue fair compensation.

Services Include

  • Car Accidents
  • Truck Accidents
  • Motorcycle Accidents
  • Pedestrian Accidents
  • Other Negligence-Related Injuries
📄

Estate Planning

Estate planning is about protecting what matters most and preparing for the future. WBS Legal Group helps individuals and families create personalized estate plans designed to safeguard their assets, provide for loved ones, and preserve their legacy.

Services Include

  • Last Wills and Testaments
  • Revocable Living Trusts
  • Powers of Attorney
  • Advance Directives for Health Care
  • Estate Plan Reviews and Updates
🚗

Traffic Violations

Traffic violations can have consequences beyond a simple fine, including points on your driving record, increased insurance premiums, and potential license suspension. WBS Legal Group provides strategic representation to help individuals understand their options, protect their driving privileges, and pursue favorable resolutions.

Services Include

  • Speeding Tickets
  • Super Speeder Violations
  • Reckless Driving
  • Driving with a Suspended License
  • Failure to Appear
  • Moving Violations
